# Blue Capital bounded release candidate — 27 October 2026

**Decision: code candidate ready for parent review as a paused release; not authorized or evidenced for launch.** This report supersedes the implementation TODOs in the first two readiness reports, while preserving those reports and commits as historical evidence. No push, deployment, production read/write, runtime-secret access, automation modification, activation or real order occurred.

## Implemented supported scope

The previous precision, partial-fill/retry, lease/concurrency, asset eligibility and ledger-derived-state fixes remain. New code closes the bounded shared-action and controlled-inception paths:

- Raw action acquisition retains all types and incomplete notices. Normalization supports ordinary domestic USD cash dividends without due bills, and exact whole-share splits with unchanged instrument identity. Confirmation binds the raw receipt/version, matching issuer terms/document hash, and two distinct authored reviews. Rejections, changed raw versions, conflicting issuer terms and unknown/incomplete notices block rather than disappear.
- Shared replay derives active and SPY quantities before the New York ex-date from economic fill order and prior approved splits. Timestamp ordering uses epoch time, including offset timestamps. An inception on or after the ex-date receives no entitlement and creates no manufactured zero settlement. Same-day ambiguous split/dividend combinations are unsupported and blocked.
- Exact-cent entitlements append as receivables at the modeled ex-date boundary. Payment appends a transfer to modeled distribution cash on payable date, without recognizing income twice. Both portfolios use the same policy; no reinvestment is modeled. Broker cash never receives these modeled amounts and cannot spend them. Whole-share splits preserve total basis and reconcile against broker positions through the existing reconciliation path.
- Stable action IDs make repeat settlement idempotent. Previously recorded amounts are checked against subsequently known history. A late fill changing entitlement causes a blocker, not silent recalculation. Corrected notices, negative compensations and other unsupported correction semantics remain blocked pending an explicit reviewed extension. Observation-time replay excludes all evidence/settlements received later.
- Coverage requires current observed issuer evidence for AAPL/MSFT/SPY, independently reviewed inventory, complete acquisition receipts, and process-date coverage from inception (or previous prelaunch coverage). An empty response alone is insufficient. Acquisition is bounded to 370 days per query and action/reconciliation history to 10,000 relevant records; reaching a processing bound fails closed. These are conservative operational bounds, not claims of unlimited history support.
- State reports operational broker-ledger NAV and separately modeled economic NAV/returns. Complete comparisons require fresh reconciliation after the relevant accounting evidence and latest snapshot, no broker drift, reviewed current action coverage, and safe-integer combined NAV. Missing/stale/incomplete values remain unavailable. Model income cannot satisfy executable buying power. Order/concentration sizing uses the smaller operational NAV; the 10% spendable-cash reserve uses operational NAV plus modeled income, conservatively respecting economic NAV without borrowing modeled cash.
- Controlled activation exists behind the unchanged hard-disable. It acquires the execution lease, verifies revision-specific evidence, refreshes real account/clock/quotes, reconciles under the same lease, and rechecks preflight. One atomic opening event contains $100,000, fresh contemporaneous whole-share SPY/residual cash, and active control/approval references. The existing unique-inception index, journal fence and database-time five-second inception guard prevent duplicate, partial or delayed/backdated opening. Same-key retry returns the original event without brokerage requests; a different key cannot reset inception.
- Authenticated MCP tools now support typed corporate-action evidence/confirmation/coverage/settlement, release attestation staging, read-only activation readiness, and hard-disabled activation. Ordinary submit_record still cannot create arbitrary ledger/control records. Staging metadata never enables trading. Attestations require actual referenced verification records; code checks structure, chronology and revision linkage, not the truth of a falsely authored claim.

## Exact external prerequisites still missing

1. **Runtime corporate-action access:** no authenticated GET to `https://data.alpaca.markets/v1/corporate-actions` was performed in production. Parent must verify the existing connection's entitlement, actual schema, pagination and receipt persistence after a reviewed deployment. The published free-plan path was investigated; no new account, credential or paid data service was added.
2. **Real action evidence/coverage:** the official journal has not received the required raw action versions, contemporaneously observed issuer documents/excerpts and hashes, matching authoritative ex/payment/rate/ratio terms, and two actual independent reviews. Current code cannot infer those facts from a payment-day holding or fabricate them from test fixtures. Missing/conflicting/unsupported terms remain blockers.
3. **Actual broker settlement:** the account has zero fills. Nonzero fill settlement and partial-fill/ambiguous-retry behavior are proven only with synthetic fixtures. This task expressly forbids orders and manufacturing fills, so it cannot supply actual broker evidence. A separately authorized validation plan is required; none was attempted here.
4. **Hosted/release evidence:** source is local and undeployed. Parent must review and deploy the exact revision with `RELEASE_REVISION` matching it, verify private hosting, persistence/readback, unattended authentication, current asset metadata and the supported corporate-action/inception workflow, and record actual named measured checks. A real authorization reference and matching release attestations are required. Generic success summaries or copied synthetic evidence do not satisfy the intended evidence standard.
5. **Fresh launch market state:** October 26 closing observations reported by the parent are stale. Future activation requires an actually open regular session, fresh IEX SPY observation and clock, flat $100,000 account, no prior order intents, current action coverage, and successful reconciliation at the actual prospective start.

These are absent external evidence/configuration/authorization, not unimplemented ordinary-dividend or atomic-inception TODOs. The implementation deliberately excludes unsupported fractional shares/cash residuals, changed-identity splits, cash in lieu, special/foreign/due-bill distributions, mergers/spinoffs/return of capital/withholding/fees, ambiguous same-day combinations and correction/compensation cases. Such events stop affected accounting/execution; they are never silently approximated. A future extension needs the specific authoritative terms and settlement semantics, plus tests and review.
