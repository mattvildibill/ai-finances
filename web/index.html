Accounting & evidence policy

Real prices, simulated execution

Alpaca paper fills are a simulation. They do not reproduce live queue position, market impact, or every source of slippage. We never substitute a quoted price for a reported fill.

A fair benchmark needs bookkeeping

SPY starts alongside the official portfolio. Distributions, fees, and corporate actions must be reconciled consistently. Unsupported adjustments pause affected accounting rather than create an invented return.

Evidence has an arrival time

Market timestamps, retrieval timestamps, and trading sessions are different. A newly retrieved old quote is still an old quote. Stale data must never authorize a trade.
